# Easy-Post Desktop — release notes 1.1.3

Master English ("what's new") copy for the version 1.1.3 store listings. House
style: no Oxford commas, no abbreviations, no pronouns, British spelling.

Applies to the Microsoft Store listing (ReleaseNotes field) and, reworded per
platform limits, the App Store "What's New". Version 1.1.3 reworks the Create
Shipment rates area — account-billed services, a signature option, carrier
grouping and insurance — so the Create Shipment screenshot changes (the other
pages are unchanged from 1.1.2).

## en-US ReleaseNotes

• Account-billed carrier services — services whose postage is invoiced to the account, such as Royal Mail Tracked and Signed For, now appear in the rates list marked "Billed to account" rather than being hidden, so every purchasable service can be chosen.
• Signature on delivery — a new option (none, signature or adult signature) requests a signature when the parcel is handed over and brings up the carrier services that provide it, including Royal Mail Signed For.
• Rates grouped by carrier — each carrier's services sit under a collapsible heading, with an "Included" column that shows at a glance whether tracking, a signature or a guaranteed delivery date is part of the service.
• Insurance — declare a value to insure a parcel for and the cover is purchased together with the label.
• Refinements and fixes throughout.
