Apps

Easy-Post apps

The desktop application for Mac and Windows, and the mobile companion for iPhone. Everything on this page comes from an app store, so it keeps itself up to date.

Easy-Post Desktop

Create shipments, compare live carrier rates and print labels, on the machine where the work happens. The store editions update themselves and need no licence key.

Windows and macOS are also offered as a direct download, for anyone who would rather not go through a store. Those builds need a paid licence key.

Easy-Post Mobile Companion

Shipment tracking on the phone, paired once with the desktop application. Free to install, and a paid Easy-Post Desktop production licence is required to pair.

The Android edition is in testing ahead of a Google Play release. More about the mobile app.
